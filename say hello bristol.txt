Say Hello Bristol is a web-based interactive pronunciation app built around a static map of Bristol, aimed at two distinct markets with very different relationships to the product. 

For tourists, international students and business visitors, it's a compelling nice-to-have — a way to get more out of time in Bristol, navigate with confidence, and engage more naturally with locals. 

For workers relocating to Bristol — international medical staff at Southmead Hospital, engineers and professionals at EDF, and others moving to the city for work — it's a genuine necessity: a practical tool that reduces anxiety, prevents miscommunication, and supports professional integration from day one. 

This distinction matters for pricing and pitch: the tourist-facing product is a one-time impulse purchase at the £2.99–£4.99 range, while the workforce market is a B2B conversation with HR and relocation teams for whom the value proposition is measurable and the budget category is entirely different. For corporate/organisational clients, the app would ideally be bundled with the existing Pronunciation Power online course.

The IPA transcriptions and audio for Bristol place names represent a genuinely original dataset not available through mainstream dictionary resources.

There is scope to create even more localised maps - e.g. of Southmead Hospital, or the UWE Filton campus. This concept is also replicable across other cities in the UK, other hospitals, other university campuses etc.



How Say Hello Bristol works:
Each card on the map shows a place name, IPA transcription, a plain-English teaching note with respelled pronunciation, and audio recorded by Jenny Dance; users can record themselves and compare to the model. 

The app will have a free tier of around 9 words and a one-time paid unlock for the full list of ~55 entries, hosted at sayhellobristol.co.uk with an about page linking back to Jenny's coaching site.  

Tap a coloured pin to open a pronunciation card. The cards that have content in this version are: Clifton, Gloucester Road, Cabot Circus, The Harbourside, Ashton Court, Ashton Gate, The Downs and The River Avon. Try the Listen button first, then Record yourself and compare.
